Shorts Crossposter

A private, single-user tool that publishes its owner's own original short explainer videos (History Decoded and related channels) to the owner's own YouTube channels, with honest titles, cited sources and an AI-use disclosure.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Uploaded videos are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How it works

  1. The owner's video workflow saves each finished original video and its details to the owner's Google Drive.
  2. A scheduled job picks the next video, checks it is original and not a near-duplicate, and writes the title, description and tags.
  3. The job uploads the video to the owner's YouTube channel with "made for kids" = No and the altered or synthetic content disclosure set, then logs the result in the owner's Google Sheet.
  4. The owner chooses the privacy setting (private, unlisted or public) for every run and reviews each upload in YouTube Studio.
