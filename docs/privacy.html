Privacy Policy - Shorts Crossposter

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Shorts Crossposter is a private, single-user tool operated by Julius Seguido (the "owner"). It publishes the owner's own original short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and has no users other than the owner.

Shorts Crossposter uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data handling by Google is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

1. Data accessed through YouTube API Services

  • Uploads videos to the owner's own channels (videos.insert) with a title, description, tags, category, language, "made for kids" setting and the altered or synthetic content disclosure.
  • Reads the owner's own channel name (channels.list) to confirm the correct channel before uploading.
  • Later: reads view counts and similar statistics of the owner's own videos (videos.list) to decide which videos to publish next.

2. Other Google data accessed

  • Google Drive (read-only): reads video files and their description files from one folder in the owner's Drive.
  • Google Sheets: reads performance numbers and writes an upload log in one spreadsheet owned by the owner.

3. How the data is used

Only to choose which of the owner's videos to upload, to write their titles and descriptions, to upload them, and to log the result. No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with data from other users.

4. Storage and retention

  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository settings, used only by the tool's automated jobs, and never shown or logged.
  • Video files are downloaded only temporarily during an upload job and deleted when the job ends.
  • The upload log (video ID, URL, title, status) is kept in the owner's Google Sheet. Any YouTube statistics stored there are refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days.
  • No cookies are used. No personal data of YouTube viewers or other users is collected.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (third-party access).
  • Within 7 days of revocation, or on request, all stored tokens are deleted and the upload log rows are removed.
  • Deletion requests: email julius.seguido@gmail.com.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Shorts Crossposter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date. Questions: julius.seguido@gmail.com
